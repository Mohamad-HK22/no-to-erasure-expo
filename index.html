ABOUT THIS EXHIBITION

One open gallery.
Space to reflect.

This local exhibition brings the Primary Documents collection from AUB Libraries’ No To Erasure project ↗ into a walkable space. The collection includes 21 works: 14 images, including photographs, artworks and maps; six PDFs with 31 pages in total; and one film.

V1 takes its cues from open museum galleries: freestanding white walls with work on both sides, a matte oak floor and soft light. Open a wall work or one of the eight tilted map displays to inspect its image, read its pages or watch the film, then continue from the same position.

How is the collection arranged?
The four themed areas follow the main project categories: Homes, Villages & Infrastructure; Education & Health; Agriculture, Nature & the Environment; and People, Culture & Heritage. The collection catalogue remains available in the page navigation.
How can I find a work?
Use the map to visit an area. In The collection, search by title, creator or keyword and filter by area or format. Work panels retain source descriptions, credits and archival details where the project supplies them.
What works without internet?
The gallery, original images, complete PDF pages, original PDFs and film are stored locally. Reading and playback work without an internet connection. Links to the project and external sources require internet.
Can I read the complete documents?
Yes. Use the page selector or arrows in each document panel. Width fits a page to the reading area, Fit shows the complete page, and + enlarges details. The original file can also be downloaded. The displaced-persons study and its published correction are linked.
