No To Erasure V1 minimal gallery — OpenVGAL runtime

Upstream project: https://github.com/lbartworks/openvgal

This package uses the OpenVGAL viewer, its Babylon.js engine, artwork
builder, navigation and picking, with locally bundled dependencies. Custom
geometry creates an open gallery with freestanding white display walls.
The original T_small_B.glb and node materials remain as upstream references.

LOCAL INTEGRATION
- Scripts, fonts, room files and textures resolve locally. The generator,
  unused room styles, recorder and external debug-console loader are omitted
  or disabled.
- The manifest places works from the AUB Libraries No To Erasure Primary
  Documents collection in four themed areas. The catalogue retains 21
  unique records: 14 images, six PDFs (31 pages) and one film. Source credits and stated rights belong to
  the individual works and are retained with their metadata.
- demo-rooms.js builds open-plan architecture and supplies the actual wall
  segments and themed-area boundaries to the live minimap. OpenVGAL still populates artworks, frames
  and plaques, and handles camera input and artwork picking.
- V1 architecture uses freestanding white walls with artwork on both sides,
  a matte oak floor and soft light. Static decoration is merged by material and is
  non-pickable. Signs and the renderer preserve their proportions when resized.
- demo-map-stands.js provides eight freestanding tilted map displays. Map
  surfaces are pickable and retain their original landscape proportions;
  housings are non-pickable and collision bodies keep walking paths clear.
- demo-controls.js adds faster movement/look and a held-Shift walking boost,
  clearing held motion on key release, blur and exhibit inspection.
- viewer.html loads demo-bridge.js and hides the stock embedded controls.
  The bridge accepts only messages from its same-origin parent.
- openvgal-viewer.js uses the bundled Droid Sans font and permits an empty
  full-room resource for the local geometry extension. The manifest selects
  the custom geometry through its layout value.
- Artwork selection emits the matching content ID without moving the camera.
  Pause clears motion, snapshots the camera pose, detaches camera input and
  suspends rendering. Resume restores the pose. Repeated pause commands do
  not overwrite it. Readiness handling covers both loading order and an
  already completed room.
- The parent website adds searchable/filterable collection records, a live
  minimap, image/PDF inspection, original downloads and a local film player.
  Closing or switching away from a film stops playback. The static host
  should support byte-range requests for video seeking.
- gallery-fullscreen.js expands the entire document so the map and sibling
  exhibit dialogs remain available. It falls back to filling the browser
  window when native fullscreen is unavailable.

RUNNING
Serve the extracted files over HTTPS and use the hosted index.html URL
in the LibGuides Media/Widget iframe. The gallery, catalogue media, complete document pages and film
are local. Optional project/source links require internet. Visits are
independent; there is no shared avatar or live voice server.

VERSION
V1 is the simpler environment introduced on 28 October 2026. The earlier
exhibition is preserved separately as V0. Original media and work IDs are
retained in both versions.
